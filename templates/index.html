💎 Upload Museum Artifacts / Jewelry

Upload an image containing multiple museum items (jewelry, necklaces, bracelets, rings, pendants, beads, or other archaeological artifacts). The pipeline detects each item, generates an individual instance mask, removes the background with transparent alpha, crops tightly, and predicts artifact classes.

⚙️ Pipeline Architecture & Constraints

🎯 Instance vs Semantic Segmentation

Every physical artifact receives its own distinct mask. Clustered or adjacent jewelry items remain separated with unique IDs (Item_01, Item_02...).

✂️ Detail & Hole Preservation

Multi-level bilateral edge refinement preserves thin necklace chains, bead links, and ring apertures with transparent center holes (alpha = 0).

💾 Standard Output Directory & JSON

Outputs are saved to outputs/detection.jpg, outputs/segmentation.jpg, outputs/objects/Item_XX.png, and outputs/results.json.
